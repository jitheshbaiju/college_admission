BEFORE YOU APPLY

Admission Information

Prepare in advance to make your application clear and complete.

Eligibility

Applicants should meet the academic qualification specified for their chosen program. Undergraduate programs generally require successful completion of 12th grade or an equivalent qualification. Program-specific prerequisites may apply, so review the course details before applying.

Review program requirements →

Required Documents

Keep clear, readable copies of the following documents ready to upload:

  • Passport-size photograph
  • Student signature (JPG or PNG)
  • Identity proof
  • 10th certificate
  • 12th certificate
  • Conduct certificate
  • Transfer certificate
  • Caste certificate for SC/ST applicants
  • Previous academic records
  • Other required certificates

Important Instructions

  • Enter correct and complete information.
  • Upload clear, readable documents.
  • Review your details before submitting.
  • Keep your application number safely.
  • Applications cannot be edited after final submission unless permitted by an administrator.
